# ADR 0010: Treat psychology as hypotheses with measurable tests

Date: 2026-11-12. Status: Proposed.

## Context
The owner requested research into human psychology and gaming theories. Frameworks, associations and experiments in other game genres cannot establish this game's fun or superiority.

## Decision
Maintain a source register distinguishing findings, frameworks, inference and chosen targets. Use SDT, MDA, GameFlow, communication/cooperation and human social-deduction research to propose falsifiable playtests. Include relevant study limitations, including population, genre and access depth. Do not use LLM deception results as evidence about human enjoyment.

Use the PT-01–PT-06 protocol: multiple distinct friend groups, repeated/counterbalanced comparisons where practical, per-player contribution/clarity/inclusion feedback and observed events. Count failures and interrupted rounds. Treat small samples as formative; do not infer market-wide superiority or exact balance from 30 rounds.

Collect minimal disclosed session/event data, with pseudonymous IDs. No default raw voice recording, automatic lie detection or vocal personality/honesty scoring. Support mute, pings and consent to optional observation. Measure replay as a voluntary choice, not pressure to stay or a reward countdown.

## Consequences
Some features will be cut despite appearing attractive in a design document. Research access gaps remain explicit. Metrics require interpretation: helping can reflect awkward controls, speech duration can reflect domination and wins can coexist with exclusion.

## Gate
Each milestone note includes conditions, evidence, counterexamples and a continue/change/stop decision. Any stronger efficacy claim requires an appropriate evaluation design rather than marketing inference.
