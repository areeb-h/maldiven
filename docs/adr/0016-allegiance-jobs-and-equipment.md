# ADR 0016: Separate allegiance, public jobs and equipment

Date: 2026-11-12. Status: Proposed; H1 equipment rules clarified, H2 public-job experiment. Extends ADR 0005/0012.

## Context

Richer social roles can create useful coordination, but fixed exclusive classes can make a disconnected specialist a veto over progress. A public medic or security label must not accidentally certify secret allegiance or grant authority over other people.

## Decision

Keep secret allegiance fixed/private, public responsibilities voluntary/transferable, and usable capabilities attached to validated equipment/actions. H1 has no mandatory job-board system. Prototype public jobs in H2 against ordinary self-organisation, with no exclusive permissions and no assignment from vocal/personality analysis.

## Alternatives

- Exclusive classes: stronger fantasy, but more progression/disconnect dependencies and balance work.
- Public jobs tied to faction: easier inference, but undermines readable uncertainty.
- No job layer: valid baseline and preferred outcome if the board adds administration.

## Consequences and validation

Equipment handover and manual recovery remain required. A label alone grants no interaction, role-view or host powers. Use SX-01/04/06 and PT-08 to inspect participation and coercion. The [social design](../blueprint/social-roles-and-agreements.md) specifies the proposal; no job-board implementation is claimed.
