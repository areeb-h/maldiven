# ADR 0012: Design a systemic station and preserve the expansion ladder

Date: 2026-11-12. Status: Proposed. Extends ADR 0005 without changing the first complete mode.

## Context
The owner requested a deeper, more ambitious plan. A small deduction slice can prove the loop, but the game's identity should support cooperation, expression and varied space situations beyond one hidden role.

## Decision
Use shared access, power, cargo, injury, communications, evidence and extraction systems as the foundation. Keep Last Departure as H1. Plan cooperative Salvage Shift, richer hangar expression, power negotiation, authored incidents, tools and eventually docking/gravity/boarding experiments as explicit horizons.

Every expansion must name the player situation it enables, existing contracts it reuses, new failure states and its smallest convincing test. No release is committed merely because an idea appears in the blueprint. The foundation schedule does not estimate every horizon.

## Consequences
A common interaction vocabulary and data boundaries require more up-front design, but can reduce bespoke content logic. System interactions produce both memorable play and unintended strategies; scenario tests and human sessions remain necessary. Multiple modes are a long-term opportunity, not required to validate H1.

## Gate
Use docs/blueprint/implementation-backlog.md expansion conditions. Shared systems enter only after corresponding authority, privacy, recovery and performance contracts pass. Preserve co-op fun when testing betrayal/combat.
